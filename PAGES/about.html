Our History

Every great journey has a beginning, and ours began with a fellowship.

For seven years, what would eventually become Jubilee Gospel Centre International existed as a fellowship, growing in purpose and laying the foundation for the ministry ahead.

On 4 October 2009, the fellowship was formally established as a church under the name Jubilee Gospel Centre International.

Our journey began at Chamley Street, where the initial gatherings were held, before the church relocated to its present headquarters at White House Street, Calabar.

Today, as City of Increase, Jubilee Gospel Centre International continues its commitment to influencing the world with the Word of God.
